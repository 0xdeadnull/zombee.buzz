> **A signal is meaningful because of its context, not merely because it is unusual.**

### Three views of the same observation

**Immediate view.** What happened now?

**Historical view.** Is this different from what normally happens?

**Relational view.** Did something else change at the same time?

## From observation to meaning

| Stage | Question |
| --- | --- |
| Observe | What happened? |
| Compare | Is it unusual? |
| Correlate | What else changed? |
| Interpret | What might it mean? |
| Act | What should happen next? |
